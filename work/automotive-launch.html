项目概述 Overview

以数字城市、空间光线和流动材质构建汽车品牌发布会影像。画面围绕建筑、产品与发布语境建立节奏和空间关系。 Digital cityscapes, spatial light and flowing materials form the visual language of an automotive launch film. Architecture and product imagery establish its rhythm and sense of space.
